# 01 - Speed-to-Lead System

**Status:** In progress

## Problem
Businesses lose customers when inquiries get a slow reply.

## What this workflow does
1. A customer submits a form.
2. Their details are saved to a Google Sheet.
3. They instantly receive an automatic email reply.
4. The business owner is alerted on Telegram or email.

## Tools
n8n (self-hosted), Google Sheets, Gmail, Telegram
